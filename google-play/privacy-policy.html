Privacy Policy

How IndoProperty collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

📅 Effective Date: 1 October 2026  ·  Last Updated: 1 October 2026

IndoProperty ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the IndoProperty mobile application and web services, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using IndoProperty, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account & Profile Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • City / location
  • Professional role (customer, broker, builder, or owner)
  • Agency or brand name (for professional accounts)
  • Profile bio or tagline (optional)
  • Profile photo (if you choose to upload one)

1.2 Property Listings

  • Property title, description, and details (type, price, area, bedrooms, etc.)
  • Property location and city
  • Property images you upload
  • Amenities and listing status

1.3 Video Reels

  • Video file and thumbnail image
  • Reel title and caption
  • Associated property (if linked)

1.4 Enquiries & Messages

  • Message content
  • Your name, email, and phone as provided in enquiry forms
  • The property or reel the enquiry relates to
  • Chat messages between you and other users

1.5 Interactions

  • Properties you save (bookmark)
  • Reels you like or save
  • Creators you follow
  • Comments you post on reels

1.6 Technical & Device Information

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • Internet connection type
  • Crash reports and error logs (for debugging only)

We do not collect precise GPS/location data. The "city" field is entered by you voluntarily. We do not use advertising identifiers or track you across other apps.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the service: Create and manage your account, display listings, deliver enquiries and messages.
  • To improve the platform: Analyse aggregated, anonymised usage patterns.
  • To ensure safety: Review reports of inappropriate content and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To send notifications: Notify you of new enquiries, likes, comments, and follows within the app.
  • To comply with law: Retain certain data as required under applicable Indian law.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising profiling or share it with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase, a cloud database service. By using IndoProperty, you consent to cross-border transfer of data. We implement: HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, industry-standard password hashing, Row Level Security (RLS) — users can only access their own data, and access-controlled cloud storage for media files.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personally identifiable information is permanently deleted. Aggregated/anonymised data may be retained indefinitely. Data required under Indian law may be retained for the legally required period.

5. Sharing of Information

  • With other users: Your public profile, property listings, and video reels are visible to other IndoProperty users.
  • With our service providers: Supabase (database and authentication). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • With law enforcement: If required by law, court order, or government authority.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: View your profile and data inside the app at any time.
  • Correction: Update your profile information in Profile Settings.
  • Deletion: Permanently delete your account via Profile Settings → Danger Zone, or by contacting support@indoproperty.in.
  • Portability: Contact us to request a copy of your data.

7. Children's Privacy

IndoProperty is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that a user under 18 has provided data, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or to exercise your data rights:

  • Email: support@indoproperty.in
  • Web: Contact & Support Page

We will respond to privacy-related requests within 30 days.
