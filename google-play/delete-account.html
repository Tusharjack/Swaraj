Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your IndoProperty account and all associated data. This action is immediate and irreversible.

📱 Fastest: Delete Inside the App

The quickest way to delete your account is directly inside the IndoProperty Android app.

  1. Open the IndoProperty app and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of Profile Settings.
  4. Find the Danger Zone section and tap Delete Account.
  5. Type DELETE in the confirmation box and confirm.
  6. Your account and all data are deleted immediately.

📂 What Data Is Deleted

All of the following is permanently and irreversibly removed:

  • 👤
    Profile & AccountName, email, phone, city, bio, profile photo
  • 🏠
    Properties & ListingsAll property posts, descriptions, and uploaded images
  • 🎬
    Video ReelsAll video reels, thumbnails, and metadata
  • 💬
    Enquiries & Chat MessagesAll enquiries sent/received and chat history
  • ❤️
    Likes, Saves & FollowsLiked reels, saved properties, followed creators
  • 🔔
    NotificationsAll notification history
  • 📁
    Uploaded FilesPhotos and videos stored on IndoProperty's servers
⚠️
Data that may be retained: Aggregated, anonymised statistics with no personal identifiers may be kept. Data required under Indian law may be retained for the legally required period. No personally identifiable information is kept beyond legal requirements.
