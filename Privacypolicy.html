Privacy Policy

IndoProperty — Real Estate for a Better Tomorrow

Effective Date: 1 October 2026

IndoProperty ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the IndoProperty mobile application and web services, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using IndoProperty, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile: name, email, phone (optional), city, role, brand name, bio, profile photo.
  • Property Listings: property details, location, images, amenities, and status.
  • Video Reels: video file, thumbnail, title, and caption.
  • Enquiries & Messages: message content and the name, email, and phone you provide.
  • Interactions: saved properties, liked/saved reels, follows, and comments.
  • Device & Technical: device type, OS version, app version, connection type, and crash logs.

We do not collect precise GPS/location data. The "city" field is entered by you voluntarily. We do not use advertising identifiers or track you across other apps.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the service: manage your account, display listings, deliver messages.
  • To improve the platform: analyse aggregated, anonymised usage patterns.
  • To ensure safety: review reports and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To send notifications: alert you to new enquiries, likes, and comments.
  • To comply with law: retain certain data as required under applicable Indian law.

We do not sell your personal data or share it with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase, a cloud database service. By using IndoProperty, you consent to cross-border transfer of data. We implement HTTPS encryption in transit, industry-standard password hashing, Row Level Security (RLS), and access-controlled cloud storage.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data while your account is active. When you delete your account, all personally identifiable information is permanently deleted. Data required under Indian law may be retained for the legally required period.

5. Sharing of Information

  • With other users: your public profile, listings, and reels are visible to other users.
  • With service providers: Supabase (database and authentication), bound to protect your data.
  • With law enforcement: if required by law, court order, or government authority.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: view your profile and data inside the app at any time.
  • Correction: update your profile information in Profile Settings.
  • Deletion: permanently delete your account via Profile Settings → Danger Zone, or email us.
  • Portability: contact us to request a copy of your data.

7. Children's Privacy

IndoProperty is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that a user under 18 has provided data, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be reflected in the "Effective Date" above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or to exercise your data rights, email us at support@indoproperty.in. We will respond to privacy-related requests within 30 days.
